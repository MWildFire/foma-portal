## Big idea

A formal letter is written to an institution or to an adult you do not know well. Its layout, its register and its closing formula show respect before the reader has read a single argument. In P1 the letter must also do a job: state a clear purpose, cover three content points and make a polite request.

## Explanation

A formal letter has a fixed layout, from top to bottom:

1. the sender's details (l'expéditeur): name and address, top left;
2. the recipient's details (le destinataire): title, institution and address, on the right;
3. the place and the date, for example « Lyon, le 12 octobre 2026 »;
4. the subject line (l'objet), « Objet : … », a few words that state the purpose;
5. the greeting (la salutation), « Madame la Directrice, » or « Madame, Monsieur, »;
6. the body, usually three or four short paragraphs: the reason for writing, the details, the request and the thanks;
7. the closing formula (la formule de politesse): « Je vous prie d'agréer, Madame, Monsieur, l'expression de mes salutations distinguées. »;
8. the signature with the full name.

The register is formal. Use vous, not tu, and avoid contractions and slang. Make polite requests with the conditional: « je souhaiterais » (I would like) and « pourriez-vous » (could you). Use the subjunctive after expressions such as « il faut que » (it is necessary that) and « je souhaite que » (I wish that). The greeting and the closing formula must match: if the letter opens with « Madame la Directrice, », it closes with « Je vous prie d'agréer, Madame la Directrice, … ».

Connectors help the reader follow the argument: « d'une part… d'autre part » (on the one hand… on the other hand), « en outre » (moreover) and « c'est pourquoi » (that is why). Keep the paragraphs in the order of the content points in the task, so that the reader finds each point quickly.

## Worked example

The task asks you to write to the headteacher about a book exchange club. Compare these two openings.

Weak: « Salut Madame, je veux créer un club de lecture, c'est super. »

Better: « Madame la Directrice, je me permets de vous écrire pour vous présenter un projet de club de lecture et d'échange de livres. »

Why the second one works:

- the greeting is formal: a title, then a comma;
- « je me permets de vous écrire » (I take the liberty of writing to you) states the purpose at once;
- there is no slang and no exclamation mark;
- the request for permission comes later, in the body, with the conditional: « je souhaiterais vous demander l'autorisation d'utiliser la salle de documentation ».

## How it is examined

The P1 letter is marked with three criteria: A Language (12 marks), B Message (12 marks) and C Conceptual understanding (6 marks). For criterion B the examiner checks that all three content points are covered, that the purpose is clear and that the text has 250 to 400 words. For criterion A, accuracy and register matter: a letter with informal words loses marks even when the content is good. For criterion C, the writer must show awareness of the reader and of the school context.

Before you write, plan the three content points in their order and check the time. A plan of five short lines is enough.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with « Salut » or with a first name, as in a personal message.
- Using tu, or informal spoken forms such as « j'veux » or « ça marche ».
- Forgetting the « Objet » line, or writing it as a full sentence instead of a short subject.
- Ending with « Bises » or « À bientôt ! » instead of the formal closing formula.
- Translating word for word: « actuellement » means « currently », not « actually », and « éventuellement » means « possibly ».
- Writing « Je veux que vous me donnez » instead of « Je veux que vous me donniez »: after « je veux que » the verb takes the subjunctive.

## Summary

- A formal letter has a fixed layout: sender, recipient, place and date, subject line, greeting, body, closing formula, signature.
- Use vous, no contractions and no slang. The greeting and the closing formula must match.
- Make polite requests with the conditional, and use the subjunctive after « il faut que » and « je souhaite que ».
- In P1 the letter must cover all three content points within 250 to 400 words.
