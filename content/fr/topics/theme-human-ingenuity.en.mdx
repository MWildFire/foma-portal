## Big idea

Human ingenuity is the third of the five IB French B themes. It asks how people invent, discover, design and create, and how these achievements change our lives. The theme covers technology and devices, inventions and research, design and art, digital media, artificial intelligence, innovation in business, and the safety of our devices and data. In Paper 1 you might write about a new invention or give a speech about technology, so you need words for explaining how things work. In Paper 2, texts about researchers and creators test whether you understand this vocabulary in context.

## Explanation

**The passive voice.** Use *être* with a past participle when the action matters more than the person who does it: *Le prototype a été testé pendant six mois* (the prototype was tested for six months). The participle agrees with the subject: *la machine a été conçue*, *les modèles ont été présentés*. In the present, use *est vendu* or *sont utilisés*.

**Agreement with *avoir*.** The participle agrees only with a direct object placed before the verb: *l'idée que la start-up a développée* (the idea that the start-up developed). When the object follows the verb, there is no agreement: *elle a fait un croquis*.

**Relative pronouns.** Use *qui*, *que*, *dont* and *où* to describe tools and places: *un outil dont on a besoin* (a tool that people need), *un laboratoire où l'on teste les idées* (a laboratory where ideas are tested).

**Purpose and the subjunctive.** Use *pour* with an infinitive when the subject stays the same, and *pour que* with the subjunctive when it changes: *ce capteur permet de mesurer la température* (this sensor measures temperature); *les ingénieurs améliorent le modèle pour que le produit soit plus sûr* (so that the product is safer).

## Worked example

A student wrote this paragraph about an invention.

Le téléphone pliable est une invention récente qui a été conçue pour concilier un grand écran et un petit format. Au début, le prototype a été testé pendant six mois par une équipe d'ingénieurs, qui a amélioré plusieurs fois la charnière. Les premiers modèles ont été présentés en public, mais ils ont dû être réparés à cause de problèmes d'écran. Aujourd'hui, l'appareil est vendu dans le monde entier. Pour les utilisateurs, c'est une avancée importante, car il tient dans une poche tout en offrant un écran de grande taille. L'idée, que la start-up a développée avec soin, a été protégée par un brevet.

**Analysis**

- Vocabulary: *une invention*, *un prototype*, *tester*, *une avancée*, *une start-up*, *un brevet*, *protéger*.
- Passive: *a été conçue* agrees with *une invention* (feminine singular); *ont été présentés* agrees with *les premiers modèles* (masculine plural); *ont dû être réparés* combines a modal verb with a passive infinitive; *est vendu* is the present passive.
- Agreement with *avoir*: in *que la start-up a développée*, *que* stands for *l'idée*, which comes before the verb, so *développée* takes the feminine ending. In *a amélioré plusieurs fois la charnière*, the object follows the verb, so there is no agreement.
- *Tout en offrant* means "while also offering".

## How it is examined

Human ingenuity can appear in any Paper 1 task or Paper 2 text. Paper 1 tasks might ask for an article about a new invention, a speech about technology, an interview with a researcher or a review of an app. Your answer is 250 to 400 words, and three criteria apply:

- Criterion A, Language (12 marks): tenses, the passive, agreements and precise vocabulary.
- Criterion B, Message (12 marks): a clear purpose, all content points and a suitable tone.
- Criterion C, Conceptual understanding (6 marks): you show how people shape technology, for example by weighing the benefits and risks of an invention.

In Paper 2 you explain phrases, choose meanings in context, or decide whether statements are true, false or not mentioned. Learn fixed expressions such as *mettre au point* and *faire ses preuves* as whole units.

## Common mistakes

- *actuel* is an adjective meaning "current" (*la technologie actuelle*). *Actuellement* is an adverb meaning "currently", so it cannot describe a noun.
- *réaliser* means "to carry out" (*réaliser une expérience*). For "to realise that", French often says *se rendre compte que*.
- *une application* is an app or the application of a rule. For a job or a university place, use *une candidature*.
- Learn the gender with the article. *Une application*, *une hypothèse* and *une vidéo* are feminine, so never write *un*. Gender can also change meaning: *le mode* is a method, *la mode* is fashion.
- With *avoir*, do not add agreement when the object follows the verb: *elle a fait un croquis*, not *faite*.

## Summary

- Human ingenuity covers technology, research, design, digital media, artificial intelligence, business, creators and safety.
- Use the passive (*être* plus a past participle) when the action matters more than the doer.
- With *être*, the participle agrees with the subject; with *avoir*, only with a direct object placed before the verb.
- Use *pour que* with the subjunctive when the subject changes.
- Learn fixed expressions such as *mettre au point* and *faire ses preuves* as whole units.
