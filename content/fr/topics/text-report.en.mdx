## Big idea

A report presents what was found, why it happened and what should be done about it. Its readers need facts they can trust, so the tone is formal and impersonal. A clear structure lets the reader find each part quickly: the aim, the findings, the causes and the recommendations.

## Explanation

A report usually follows a fixed layout:

- **Title.** It says what the report is about, for example « Rapport sur la consommation d'eau dans l'établissement ».
- **Introduction.** It states the aim (l'objectif) and the method (la méthode): what was measured, when and how.
- **Numbered sections.** Headings such as 1. Contexte, 2. Constats and 3. Recommandations divide the report into clear parts, and each part does one job.
- **Conclusion.** It sums up the main point in two or three sentences and may offer to present the findings.

The register is formal and impersonal. Use on, the passive and expressions with il est: « On constate que… », « Les relevés sont présentés… », « Il est important de… ». Give figures in words or as approximations: « environ », « près de », « une vingtaine de ». For recommendations, use « Il est recommandé de » with an infinitive, or « Nous recommandons que » with the subjunctive: « Nous recommandons que les robinets soient réparés. » Avoid personal opinions such as « je pense que » in a formal report.

## Worked example

Task: a student committee reports on water use at school.

Observation, with an approximate figure:
« Pendant trois semaines, le comité a relevé les compteurs chaque lundi. On constate que près de la moitié de l'eau part dans les sanitaires. »

Cause, linked to the observation:
« Ce constat s'explique en partie par des robinets qui ne se ferment pas automatiquement. »

Recommendation, with a clear action:
« Il est recommandé de réparer les fuites dans les plus brefs délais. »

The three sentences move from fact to cause to action. This is the logic that examiners look for in the content points.

## Visual

| Section | What it contains | Useful phrase |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction | the aim and the method | « Ce rapport présente… » |
| 1. Contexte | the situation and the data collected | « Le comité a relevé… » |
| 2. Constats | the findings and their causes | « On constate que… » |
| 3. Recommandations | concrete proposals | « Il est recommandé de… » |
| Conclusion | the main point and the next step | « En résumé, … » |

## How it is examined

Paper 1 is worth 30 marks: 12 for Language (Criterion A), 12 for Message (Criterion B) and 6 for Conceptual understanding (Criterion C). For a report, Criterion B checks the three content points: what the committee observed, the causes, and the recommendations. Criterion A checks the formal register, the impersonal forms and the subjunctive after « Nous recommandons que ». Criterion C looks for an understanding of the wider issue: why water use matters for the whole school community, not only the figures.

Keep to 250 to 400 words. Plan for about 5 minutes, write for about 25 minutes and check for about 10 minutes.

## Common mistakes

- **Personal tone.** « Je pense que l'eau est gaspillée » weakens a report. Write « On constate un gaspillage d'eau important. »
- **Vague figures.** « Beaucoup d'eau part » is less useful than « environ la moitié de l'eau ».
- **Wrong mood after que.** « Nous recommandons que les robinets sont réparés » is wrong. After que use the subjunctive: « Nous recommandons que les robinets soient réparés. »
- **Recommendations without causes.** Each recommendation should answer a cause from the findings. Otherwise the reader cannot see the logic.
- **Missing structure.** A report without numbered sections or a conclusion is hard to read quickly.

## Summary

- A report has a title, an introduction with the aim and the method, numbered sections and a conclusion.
- Use a formal, impersonal register with on, the passive and il est.
- Give approximate figures in words, such as environ, près de and une vingtaine de.
- Recommendations use « Il est recommandé de » or « Nous recommandons que » with the subjunctive.
- Link every recommendation to a cause in the findings.
