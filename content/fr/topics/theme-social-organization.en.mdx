## Big idea

Social organization is the fourth IB French B theme. It covers how people live together: school and work, voluntary groups, rights and rules, public services, health, inequality, the media and festivals. In Paper 1 you need words for institutions, problems and solutions. In Paper 2 the texts describe social problems and the people who respond to them, so you must understand this vocabulary in context.

## Explanation

**The subjunctive after il faut que, bien que and pour que.** Form the subjunctive from the *ils* present: remove *-ent* and add *-e*, *-es*, *-e*, *-ions*, *-iez* or *-ent*: *que nous parlions*, *que vous finissiez*. Irregular verbs include *être* (*que je sois*), *avoir* (*qu'il ait*) and *faire* (*que nous fassions*).

**No subjunctive with il faut and an infinitive.** *Il faut prendre rendez-vous* has no *que*, because there is no second subject.

**Opinion with penser que.** After an affirmative *je pense que*, use the indicative: *Je pense que la mesure est juste.* After a negative, use the subjunctive: *Je ne pense pas que la mesure soit juste.*

**The passive with être.** Rules and rights are often passive: *Les droits des travailleurs sont protégés par la loi.* The past participle agrees with the subject.

**The conditional for suggestions.** *Il serait utile de créer un club de devoirs* is softer and more formal than the present.

**Nouns for formal writing.** *la lutte contre le chômage* (the fight against unemployment) and *la réduction des inégalités* (the reduction of inequalities).

## Worked example

A student wrote this paragraph about a community project.

Dans notre quartier, un groupe d'élèves a lancé un projet de potager collectif. Bien qu'il y ait peu d'espace dans la cour, les participants ont trouvé un coin ensoleillé près de la bibliothèque. Il faut que chacun fasse sa part, mais le projet fonctionne déjà très bien : les voisins viennent arroser les plantes et les familles reçoivent des légumes gratuitement. Pour que tout le monde puisse participer, nous organisons aussi un atelier de cuisine le samedi. Certains élèves ont même créé un petit site internet pour présenter le projet. Je pense que ce projet change la façon dont les gens se parlent.

**Analysis**

- Subjunctive after *bien que*: *Bien qu'il y ait peu d'espace* (although there is little space). *Il y ait* is the subjunctive of *il y a*.
- Subjunctive after *il faut que*: *Il faut que chacun fasse sa part* (everyone must do their share). The indicative *fait* would be wrong.
- Subjunctive after *pour que*: *Pour que tout le monde puisse participer* (so that everyone can take part), from *pouvoir*.
- Indicative after an affirmative *je pense que*: *Je pense que ce projet change la façon dont les gens se parlent* (no subjunctive).
- Vocabulary: *un potager collectif* (community vegetable garden), *faire sa part* (to do one's share), *un coin ensoleillé* (sunny spot).
- Agreement: *créé* does not agree, because *un petit site internet* comes after the verb.

## How it is examined

Social organization can appear in any Paper 1 task or Paper 2 text. A Paper 1 answer is 250 to 400 words, judged on three criteria:

- Criterion A, Language (12 marks): subjunctives, the passive, agreements and vocabulary.
- Criterion B, Message (12 marks): a clear purpose, all content points and a suitable tone.
- Criterion C, Conceptual understanding (6 marks): you show that you understand social issues, for example how a problem affects different groups.

In Paper 2 you find words in context, explain fixed expressions, and decide whether a statement is true, false or not mentioned. Learn fixed expressions such as *être au chômage* as whole units.

## Common mistakes

- *assister à* means "to attend" (a meeting or a concert), not "to assist"; use *aider* for "to help".
- *une location* is a rental of a flat or a car. For a place, use *un lieu*.
- *la chance* means luck; *le hasard* means chance or coincidence. Say *le hasard*, not *la hasard*.
- After an affirmative *penser que*, use the indicative: *je pense qu'il a raison*, not *je pense qu'il ait raison*.
- After *il faut que*, use the subjunctive: *il faut que tu viennes*, not *il faut que tu viens*.
- *éventuellement* means "possibly", not "eventually".

## Summary

- Social organization covers school, work, rights, services, health, inequality, the media and festivals.
- Use the subjunctive after *il faut que*, *bien que* and *pour que*.
- Use the indicative after an affirmative *je pense que*, and the subjunctive after a negative.
- Learn fixed expressions such as *faire du bénévolat* and *être au chômage* as whole units.
- Use nouns such as *la lutte contre* for a formal tone.
