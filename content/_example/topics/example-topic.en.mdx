## Big idea

Demand describes how buyers respond to price. Price elasticity of demand measures how strongly they respond.

## Explanation

Demand is the quantity of a good that buyers are willing and able to purchase at each price, per period of time. The law of demand says that, other things being equal, a higher price means a lower quantity demanded, so the demand curve slopes downwards.

Demand is one half of the market; <Term id="supply"/> is the other. Their interaction sets the market price, and a change in price moves along the curve rather than shifting it.

Elasticity turns this into a number that can be compared across goods:

$$
\text{PED} = \frac{\%\Delta Q_d}{\%\Delta P}
$$

<Formula id="ped"/>

<Callout kind="define">Elastic demand: the absolute value of PED is greater than 1. Inelastic demand: less than 1. Unit elastic: exactly 1.</Callout>

## Worked example

A coffee shop raises its price from 3.00 to 3.30 AED (+10%). Daily sales fall from 400 to 360 cups (-10%).

<Steps>
<Step>Percentage change in quantity: (360 - 400) / 400 = -10%.</Step>
<Step>Percentage change in price: (3.30 - 3.00) / 3.00 = +10%.</Step>
<Step>PED = -10% / 10% = -1, so demand is unit elastic.</Step>
</Steps>

<Reveal title="Why do we drop the minus sign?">Demand slopes downwards, so PED is always negative. Answers quote the absolute value unless the question asks for the sign.</Reveal>

## How it is examined

Calculate questions give marks for each step: the percentage change in quantity, the percentage change in price, then the ratio with its sign or units. Explain questions need a reason linked to the number, not just a definition.

## Common mistakes

- Dividing by the new value instead of the original value when working out a percentage change.
- Dropping the units or the sign from the final answer without saying why.
- Calling a good inelastic only because it is expensive; elasticity depends on responsiveness, not on the price level.

## Summary

- Demand is the quantity bought at each price; the law of demand gives a downward slope.
- PED is the percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in price.
- |PED| above 1 means elastic; below 1, inelastic; exactly 1, unit elastic.
- When demand is inelastic, a price rise increases total revenue.
- Always say whether you are quoting the sign or the absolute value.
